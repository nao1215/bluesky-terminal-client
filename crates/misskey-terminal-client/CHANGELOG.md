# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and this project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- msky is developed in [nao1215/social-terminal-clients](https://github.com/nao1215/social-terminal-clients), as the crate `crates/misskey-terminal-client` of its Cargo workspace, beside bsky. Its releases from there are tagged `msky-vX.Y.Z`; the files they carry keep their names (`msky-vX.Y.Z-<target>.tar.gz`, `checksums.txt`, `msky-vX.Y.Z-THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.html`), and the cosign certificate names `.github/workflows/release-msky.yml` of that repository.
- The full-screen client lays text out, edits text, scales pictures, reads keys, fills the clipboard, picks its colors and checks the terminal with the workspace's `terminal-kit` crate, the code bsky uses, in place of msky's own copies of it.

### Fixed

- A setting too long for the settings screen, such as a download folder, is cut between whole emoji: its start could be left as half a flag, a skin tone or the end of a family.
- `q` right after a write, such as `4` marking the notifications read, waits up to 3 seconds for it to reach the server: msky ended before the request left, and the notifications stayed unread.
- A note whose line ends with a space just where it wraps no longer shows a blank line under that line, and a line of spaces wider than the column is one blank line rather than two, as bsky shows them: msky's copy of the text layout lacked bsky's fix.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-28

### Fixed

- An MP4 with a box msky cannot read (smaller than its header, longer than the file, or a 64-bit size cut short) is refused rather than uploaded: the walk stopped at such a box, and a user data or location box after it, or after one inside `moov`, went up as it was.
- The client and `msky post --no-card` read a note's MFM with the grammar of mfm-js 0.28.0, the parser of Misskey's web client, rule for rule: `msky post --no-card` no longer makes an unclosed `$[x2 ` around a link close on the `]` it adds, which showed the note otherwise than it was written (`$[x2 \n$[x2 $[x2 [label](https://e.example/c)]`).
- Markup that never closes (`**bold`, `$[x2 big`, `<small>`) is shown as written with all that follows it, links included, as Misskey shows it, and `msky post --no-card` leaves the links in it as they are, since Misskey shows no preview for them.
- A quote (`> `), `*italic*`, `_italic_`, `~~strike~~` and `***big***` are read as Misskey reads them: the client shows what they hold (a quote's lines after the `>` they start with), and `msky post --no-card` silences the links in a quote.
- A `<center>` is read only where a line starts, and `<sup>` and `<sub>` are shown as written, as Misskey shows them, where the client took their tags away anywhere.
- A function Misskey's web client does not draw (`$[unknown text]`, `$[font text]` without a family) is shown as `$[unknown text]`, and a `$[ruby ...]` of more than one part shows its last part as the reading only when it is text, as Misskey shows them.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-28

### Added

- The client turns on bracketed paste, as bsky does: pasted text goes into the field with focus as one piece (the composer, the profile editor, a report's comment, the search box, a chat message, a settings row, a column's search, the reaction filter), where before it came as keys and a pasted `q` or `n` acted as one. Nothing is taken while a note, a message or the profile is on its way.
- Custom emoji are drawn as their pictures in display names too (a note's author, the renote and reply lines, the Profile header, the accounts found, the notifications), in the note a notification is about, in a reaction a notification names, in the line of a quoted note, in a profile's description and in the description of an account a search finds, where they stayed as `:name:` before.
- On kitty, the counts after a reaction with VS16 (`🏛️`, `❤️`) no longer stay on screen after the tab changes, nor show twice when it comes back: ratatui wrote the cell after such a character where the terminal had already moved past it, so the rest of the line was one cell to the right of where it was meant to be.
- The client's thread and profile ask for what they need side by side: `v` waited for up to 13 requests one after another (a reply, its replies and the replies under them) and now waits for at most four rounds, and a profile asks for the account and its notes at once, your own without asking `i` first. With every answer 100 ms away, a thread of ten replies with replies of their own shows in 0.42 s instead of 1.33 s, and a profile in 0.11 s instead of 0.21 to 0.32 s.
- The client's Profile tab draws its header as bsky's: a big avatar beside the name, the address, the counts in bold, and how you are related (following, requested, follows you, muted, blocked, `f to toggle`); your own shows `[ e Edit profile ]` and `[ s Settings ]` buttons, and `e` edits it even when it was opened from a list. `m` on someone's profile opens the Chat tab with the conversation with them, `.` lists what `f`, `M`, `B`, `m` and `enter` do to the account shown or found, and esc says where it goes back to.
- The client's tabs keep a space between them, the Chat tab counts the conversations with a message not read, the notifications not read when their tab is shown keep a dot, and your own reaction and renote show in colour in a note's counts, as bsky's like and repost do. The chat says "you" for your own messages, where the conversation starts, and how to start one.
- The client's screens are in Japanese too where the language is Japanese: the notes, the profile and search headers, the notifications, the chat, the composer, the login form, the viewer, the help and the error box, where only the keys and the settings were before.
- `msky` without a command opens a full-screen client, built as bsky's: the home, local and global timelines, the next page by `untilId` near the end, the thread of a note with `v` (what it answers, and two levels of replies), the notifications, the files of a note full screen with `space` and saved with `d` (as `msky download` names them), the address opened with `o` and copied with `c`, `R` to load again, `?` for the keys and `.` for what they do to the note. The tabs keep bsky's numbers and keys; its Chat, Search and Profile tabs, the composer, and reactions are for a later version.
- Avatars and pictures are drawn in the terminal with kitty graphics, sixel or iTerm2 inline images, by bsky's picture pipeline (downloads and encodes on threads of their own, a disk cache in `MSKY_CACHE_DIR`, `MSKY_GRAPHICS` to name the protocol); a terminal with none runs the client as text, and `space` opens the note in the browser.
- Files go up without their metadata, as bsky's do, from `msky post`, the client's composer and the profile editor: a photo's EXIF (its GPS position among it), XMP, IPTC and comments, and a video's user data, metadata, location and XMP boxes and handler names, or a WebM's tags. A sideways photo is turned upright first; pictures otherwise keep their bytes and an MP4 keeps every offset. A file that says it is a picture or video but cannot be read as one is refused rather than uploaded with what it may carry.
- The client reads ahead the thread of the note the selection rests on for 400 ms, as bsky's does, so `v` shows it at once, and the Notifications tab counts the notifications not read yet, asked once a minute from the account (`unreadNotificationsCount`, `(•)` where a server only says there are some).
- `msky report TARGET --reason R [--comment TEXT] [--yes]` and the client's `!`, as bsky's `report`: an account, or a note's author with the note's address, is reported to the server's moderators with `users/report-abuse`, the reason starting the comment. Nothing is sent until it is confirmed: `--yes` or `y` on a terminal for the command, `y` after `enter` in the client.
- Columns on the Home tab, as bsky's on its Timeline tab: `+` on the Home tab offers the home timeline, the account's antennas, lists and followed or favorited channels, the notifications, your own notes and a search, and adds the one chosen beside the home timeline, which becomes a column of its own; the Home tab shows them side by side from then on, as many as fit (at least 36 cells each), and says how many are off screen. `←` `→` (or `H` `L`) choose the column the keys act on, `<` `>` move it, `R` loads it again, `x` removes it once `y` confirms, and with the last one gone the home timeline is shown alone again. Every key of a note works on the column's selected note, and a reaction, renote, deletion or note posted shows in the columns that hold it. The columns are kept in `settings.json` per account, and a column of a kind a newer version added is left out and kept.
- The client's profile editor, as bsky's: `e` on your own Profile tab edits the name, the description, and a new avatar and banner (a path, or `ctrl+o` to choose a picture), and `ctrl+s` saves with `i/update`, uploading the pictures first and sending only what changed. `msky login` asks for `write:account` and `write:report-abuse` too.
- The client's settings screen (`s`), theme list (`T`) and languages, as bsky's: bsky's themes, previewed as the selection moves (`terminal` stays the default, and `NO_COLOR` draws in `monochrome`), English and Japanese by bsky's mechanism (the settings' `language`, else the locale, else English), the download folder and the browser (the variables win for a run, and the screen says so), kept in `settings.json` beside what this version does not know. The reactions given lately are kept there too, and offered first on the next run.
- The client's Chat tab (7), as bsky's: Misskey's chat with accounts and rooms (`chat/history`, `chat/messages/user-timeline` and `room-timeline`, `create-to-user` and `create-to-room`), `enter` to open a conversation, `i` to write and `enter` to send, `esc` back, read again every 15 s while shown. Chat is asked for when the client starts, so on a server without chat, or for a role that may not chat, the Chat tab is not shown at all (misskey.io, for one, has no chat routes although its meta says chat is available), and switching account asks again. `msky chat [ACTOR] [TEXT]` does the same from the command line, as bsky's `chat`. `msky login` asks for `read:chat` and `write:chat` too.
- Custom emoji are drawn as their pictures in the client, in a note's text and its reaction counts: the server's own from `/api/emojis`, another server's from the note's `emojis` and `reactionEmojis`; without pictures their names stay. `e`'s picker shows the recent reactions and Misskey's default ones, and typing searches the server's custom emoji by name, each with its picture.
- The viewer plays a note's video, without sound, as bsky's does: an MP4 with H.264 is downloaded (up to 100 MB), its pictures are taken from its sample table and decoded with OpenH264 built into msky, and paced by its own clock; `r` plays it again. A WebM (VP8, VP9, AV1) or an MP4 of another codec says why it cannot be played (OpenH264 decodes H.264 only), with `d` and `o` to save or open it.
- The Misskey side of the rest of bsky's commands: `msky thread NOTE`, `msky profile [ACTOR]`, `msky reactions NOTE` (alias `likes`), `msky renotes NOTE` (alias `reposts`), `msky like NOTE [--reaction R]` (❤ by default, replacing a reaction given before) and `msky unlike NOTE`, `msky repost NOTE` and `msky unrepost NOTE` (aliases `renote`, `unrenote`), `msky delete NOTE`, `msky mute`, `unmute`, `mutes`, `msky block`, `unblock`, `blocks`; and in the client `M` and `B` (`y` confirms a block) on the Profile tab and the accounts found. `msky login` asks for `read:mutes`, `write:mutes`, `read:blocks` and `write:blocks` too.
- Each timeline is a tab of its own in the client, and the tabs are always the same, as bsky's: 1 Home, 2 Local, 3 Global, 4 Notifications, 5 Search, 6 Profile, 7 Chat, `tab` and `shift+tab` going round them, each tab keeping its place, its pages and its next page, loaded the first time it is shown, and `R` loading only the one shown. Social is not a tab (`msky timeline social` reads it), and a `tabs` key an earlier build wrote in `settings.json` is ignored without a word and kept when the settings are saved.
- The client's Search tab and Profile tab, as bsky's: `/` or `i` types the query and `t` switches between notes and accounts, each paged; `enter` opens the profile of a note's author, an account found, or a notification's sender, and `esc` goes back; the profile shows the account's header (its description as plain text) over its notes, and `f` follows or unfollows it, or takes back a request.
- The client writes, with bsky's keys: `n` a new note, `r` a reply, `Q` a quote, in a composer with bsky's keys (`ctrl+s`, `ctrl+o` and its file browser for up to four pictures or one video with alt text, `tab`, `ctrl+x`, `esc`) and Misskey's content warning (`ctrl+w`) and visibility (`ctrl+p`); `l` reacts ❤ or takes the reaction back, `e` chooses one of Misskey's default reactions; `b` renotes or takes the renote back; `D` deletes your own note after `y`; the notifications are marked read when their tab shows them. A note longer than the server's `maxNoteTextLength` is refused before its files are uploaded.
- `msky login` asks for `write:reactions` and `write:notifications` too. A login saved before cannot react or mark notifications read: the client says to log in again when asked to, and leaves the notifications unread.
- MFM is drawn in the client as plain text, as Misskey's client shows what it draws less the drawing: a function (`$[x2 ...]`, `$[tada ...]`, `$[fg.color=f00 ...]`, nested ones too) is its content, `$[ruby base reading]` is `base(reading)`, `<center>`, `<small>`, `<i>` and the like are what they hold, `**bold**` and `<b>` are bold, a link with a label is its label, and `<plain>` is kept as written. Code and custom emoji are left as written; MFM that does not close is shown as typed. The commands print the text as the server sends it.
- After the viewer closes on kitty, the whole screen is drawn again: drawn on the difference alone, the screen kept the end of a line of the notes shown before the viewer beside the pictures after scrolling, and an emoji gone from a line.
- A content warning is folded under its reason until `w` opens it; a renote shows the note it renotes under `⟳ renoted by`, a quote a line for the note it quotes, a reply a line for the note it answers. A file marked sensitive is drawn as any other, with a `⚠ sensitive` mark. Custom emoji are shown by name.
- Without an account the client opens on a login form, as bsky's does: a server chosen from Misskey Hub's list (or any host typed after `/`), then MiAuth in the browser, and the login saved as `msky login` saves it. `esc` reads the server as a visitor instead, with home saying how to log in and the local and global timelines a key away.
- `A` in the client lists the accounts logged in: `enter` switches to one and its server and keeps it in use for the next start, `x` then `y` logs one out, `a` logs another in.
- `msky accounts` lists the accounts logged in as bsky's does, the one in use marked (`--json`: `{"id", "username", "host", "service", "current"}`); `--use HOST` makes another the one in use.
- `msky servers` lists servers to log in to: the servers of Misskey Hub's list that run Misskey, the most accounts first, else the few msky carries (checked against each one's `meta`). `msky login` without `--host`, in a terminal, lists them and asks which one.
- `msky follows [ACTOR]` and `msky followers [ACTOR]`, with bsky's options (`-n`, 50 by default), yours without ACTOR, paged by `untilId`; `--pending` lists your requests not yet accepted (`following/requests/sent`) or those waiting for your approval (`following/requests/list`).

- `msky post TEXT` (`-` reads it from stdin): posts a note with the options of bsky's `post`, `--image` up to four times or one `--video`, `--alt` for each, `--reply NOTE` and `--quote NOTE` (a quote renote), and Misskey's `--cw TEXT` and `--visibility public|home|followers` (public by default). The files are uploaded to the drive (`drive/files/create`, the alt text as their caption) and attached; the note's address is printed, and with `--json` `{"uri", "id"}` as bsky prints `{"uri", "cid"}`. A text longer than the server's `maxNoteTextLength` (from its `meta`, else 3000), a content warning over 100, an alt text over 512, more than four pictures, pictures and a video together, more `--alt` than files, an empty note, a note on another server, a file over the account's `maxFileSizeMb`, and a login that may not write are exit 1 before anything is sent; a file that cannot be read is exit 3. Writes are sent once.
- `msky post --dry-run`: prints the note it would send and the preview the server makes of the first address Misskey's web client shows one under (`GET /url?url=...&lang=ja-JP`: title, description, thumbnail, site name), and sends nothing. A preview the server cannot make is exit 4, so a CI job stops before posting.
- `msky post --no-card` writes the links as MFM silent links (`?[label](address)`), which Misskey's clients show no preview of. Misskey sends no card with a note: each viewer's client asks its own server for a preview of the addresses in the text; README's Link previews says how, with the sources.
- `msky follow ACTOR` and `msky unfollow ACTOR` (`following/create`, `following/delete`, and `following/requests/cancel` for a request not yet approved), with `--json` as bsky's: `{"id"}`, `{"id", "already": true}`, `{"deleted"}`. An account of another server is asked again after the follow, and one whose server has not accepted yet is said to be pending (`"requested": true`).
- `MSKY_TOKEN`: an access token made in the server's settings, used in place of the saved login on `MSKY_HOST` (or `--host`, else misskey.io), for CI. It is never printed, and a token the server turns down says what permissions it needs.
- `msky author ACTOR`: an account's notes, newest first, read a page of up to 100 at a time with `untilId` until `-n` notes came (20 by default), each note once. ACTOR is `name`, `@name`, `@name@host`, or the account's address. The options are those of bsky's `author`: `--filter with-replies` (default), `no-replies`, `media` (notes with files), `video` and `threads` (both told by msky from the notes, as Misskey cannot filter by them), and `--reposts` to keep renotes, each under a `⟳ renoted by @name` line.
- `msky note NOTE`: one note, by its ID or its address.
- `msky search QUERY`: the notes that match, newest first, paged like `author`.
- `msky download NOTE`: saves the files attached to a note and to the note it renotes or quotes, as `<note id>-<n>.<ext>`, in `-o DIR`, `MSKY_DOWNLOAD_DIR`, or `msky` in the Downloads folder, and prints each path (with `--json`, a line of `note`, `kind`, `url` and `path` each). A note without files is a usage error. A file is never written over (the next is saved as `name (1).ext`), files over 200 MB and addresses other than http and https are refused, and a file that could not be finished is removed. `msky author ACTOR --download` (or `-o DIR`) saves the files of the notes it read instead of printing them.
- `--host` and `MSKY_HOST` choose the server, misskey.io by default; an address given for a note or an account is read on its own server.
- `--json` prints one note per line in the same shape for every command: the time, the text and the content warning, the address, the reactions with their total, the renote and reply counts, the files with their address, type and size, and the account with its host.
- `msky login`: logs in with MiAuth, asking to read (`read:account`, `read:notifications`, `read:following`, `read:reactions`), to post (`write:notes`, `write:drive`) and to follow (`write:following`), and saves what it asked for with the token. A login saved by a build that asked to read only makes `post`, `follow` and `unfollow` say to log in again, before anything is sent. It prints the address to open on the server, opens it in the web browser when stderr is a terminal (`MSKY_BROWSER` names the program), and asks `miauth/<session>/check` every two seconds until the access is allowed. Access not allowed within `--timeout` seconds (300 by default), or denied, is exit 1 and nothing is saved. msky never handles a password.
- The token is saved per server, as bsky saves its sessions: `accounts/<host>.json` in `MSKY_CONFIG_DIR` or `msky` in the platform's config folder, owner-only (0600) on Unix and written atomically, with `accounts.json` naming the account in use. Without `--host` or `MSKY_HOST`, msky asks the server of the account in use.
- `msky logout` removes the token of the account in use or of the one on `--host`, and `--all` every one, a broken login file included.
- `msky whoami`: the account logged in on the server (`i`), its address, its counts and its description; `--json` prints a note's `user` with them.
- `msky timeline [home|local|social|global]`: a timeline, paged as `author` pages, each note once, renotes left out unless `--reposts`. `home` and `social` need a login; `local` and `global` are read as a visitor without one.
- `msky notifications`: the account's notifications, newest first, paged the same way and left unread on the server (`markAsRead: false`); `--json` prints `id`, `createdAt`, `type`, `user`, `reaction`, and `note` in the shape every command prints.
- `author`, `note`, `search` and `download` send the token saved for the server they ask, and only to that server; without one they read as a visitor as before.
- A token the server no longer takes, a permission it lacks, and a timeline the server keeps from you each say what to do.
- A call the server turns away for being asked too often (HTTP 429, `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`) or that a busy gateway drops is tried again up to three times, after the wait `Retry-After` names (a minute at most) or 1, 2 and 4 seconds.
- A video in a note is drawn in the client's timelines, columns, threads and profiles by the poster the server made of it (`thumbnailUrl`), in the box a picture gets and in the note's order beside its pictures, under the `▶ 1 video` line, as bsky draws a video's thumbnail; before, it was that line alone. A video without a poster stays the line, and `space` opens the viewer as before.
- The client's Chat tab draws custom emoji as pictures in the conversations and their messages, as a note's (the sender's own, else the server's), where they stayed as `:name:`, and names whom a conversation is with by display name and address, as bsky's does, where it showed the address alone; the list no longer repeats their name before their last message.
- On Windows, the client's composer, chat and profile editor keep emoji and other characters outside the Basic Multilingual Plane typed into them (`👨‍👩‍👧`, `🇯🇵`, `🐈‍⬛`): the console hands each as two surrogates, which crossterm dropped, so a note typed as `こんにちは 👨‍👩‍👧` was sent with only its joiners. msky now reads those from the console itself, as bsky does.

### Fixed

- A video whose MP4 gives a box a 64-bit size near the largest no longer leaves the viewer loading forever: the player stopped on it, and now the viewer says the file is broken.
- A note whose reaction counts add up past the largest number no longer stops the client or prints a wrong total: the total stops at the largest.
- `msky post --no-card` no longer leaves a preview under a link that a function holds (`$[x2 https://...]`) or that comes after a `[` closing nothing on its line: the `[` is written `<plain>[</plain>`, which reads the same.
- An address in angle brackets (`<https://...>`) is shown in the client without its brackets, as Misskey's web client shows it, where it kept them.
- A function, `**bold**` or a tag in a note now closes after an address in it rather than at a `]`, `**` or `</tag>` inside the address, as Misskey reads it, and `msky post --no-card` keeps such a note reading as it did.
- `msky post --no-card` no longer leaves a preview under a link after a `<plain>` that never closes: the `</plain>` it adds closed it, and the `<plain>` is now written `<plain><</plain>plain>`, which reads the same.
- An address whose brackets hold what reads as MFM elsewhere (`https://a.test/([x](https://b.test))`) is shown as written in the client, as Misskey reads it, where the label inside it was drawn as a link.
- A `$[` that starts no function (`$[x2 $[ab:c] 後]`) is text in the client, as Misskey reads it, so the `]` after it closes the function around it.
- Code in a function (`` $[x2 `]` 後] ``) no longer closes it at a `]` inside the code, as Misskey reads it.
- `msky post --no-card` no longer makes `http://` before a link into an address of its own with a preview: the `?` it adds is put after an empty `<plain></plain>` there.
- `msky post --no-card` no longer leaves a preview under a link after a `<https://` that never closes: the `>` it adds closed it, and the `<` is now written `<plain><</plain>`, which reads the same.
- A code block opens only at the start of a line in the client, as Misskey reads it: right after `$[x2 ` or another opening it is text, where its content was kept as written.
- A function inside `**bold**` or a tag is read whole in the client, as Misskey reads it, so a `**` or `</tag>` inside the function no longer closes the bold or the tag.
